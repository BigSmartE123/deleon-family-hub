Privacy Policy

DeLeon Family Hub

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Purpose

DeLeon Family Hub is a private application developed for personal household use.

Information Accessed

With authorization, the application accesses Google Calendar event titles and dates to display family schedules.

How Information Is Used

Calendar information is used to display events on a private family dashboard. The application does not create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.

Storage and Security

Google OAuth credentials are stored as Cloudflare Worker secrets. Calendar information is retrieved for display and is not intentionally stored in a permanent database.

The dashboard requires authentication.

Information Sharing

The application does not sell calendar information or share it with advertisers. Google and Cloudflare process information as necessary to operate the application.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke Google account access at Google Account Connections .

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application administrator using the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
